# Cataclysm Mod

The Cataclysm Mod is a Minecraft Bedrock mod that triggers a cataclysm after three days of gameplay. The player takes on the role of an avatar among seven initial shelters, with only four being functional at the start. Accompanied by a cute koala pet, the player must embark on a journey to protect these shelters. The mod introduces hybrid animals and insects, and the player gains three unique powers: creating illusions, flying, and exiting their body as a spirit. Additionally, an anomaly corrupts villagers, adding a layer of danger and challenge to the game.

## Install

1. Download the Cataclysm Mod from the GitHub repository.
2. Place the mod file in the `mods` folder of your Minecraft Bedrock installation.
3. Launch Minecraft Bedrock and enable the mod in the settings.

## Use

1. Start a new Minecraft Bedrock world.
2. The cataclysm will trigger automatically after three in-game days.
3. Explore the world, interact with the four initial shelters, and protect them from the impending cataclysm.
4. Gain powers by progressing through the story:
   - **Illusion Power**: Create illusions to deceive enemies or solve puzzles.
   - **Flying Power**: Soar through the skies to reach high places or escape danger.
   - **Spirit Power**: Exit your body as a spirit to navigate areas or solve puzzles from a different perspective.
5. Encounter and tame the koala pet to have a companion throughout your journey.
6. Interact with hybrid animals and insects, and use your powers to protect the shelters and survive the cataclysm.
